## LinkedIn

### Post 1 — Feature announcement
```
Selamat Hari Raya is almost here — and I built something for the family 🌙

**Introducing Kumpul Duit Raya** — a free, offline-first web app to track duit raya collection for your whole family.

Whether you have 5 kids or 15 grandchildren, this keeps everything organised.

**How to get started:**

⚙️ **Step 1 — Create your event**
Go to Settings → Events → tap "Create Event"
Give it a name (e.g. "Raya 2026"), pick an emoji and a theme colour, then set it as Active.

⚙️ **Step 2 — Add family members**
Go to Settings → Members → tap "Add Member"
Enter their name, pick an avatar, and assign a role (child, parent, grandparent, or relative).

🏆 **Step 3 — Check the Leaderboard**
Head to the Leaderboard page to see real-time rankings of who has collected the most duit raya!

**Why I built this:**
Every Raya, someone in the family asks "berapa dia dapat?" — now we have a proper answer 😄

No account needed. No data leaves your phone. Works offline.

👉 Try it free: kumpul-duit-raya.naimsolong.com
```
